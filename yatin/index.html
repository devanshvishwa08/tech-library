How it works

Every device gets a paper trail

From the moment you drop off a gadget to the moment its parts end up in a student's STEM kit, here's exactly what happens to it.

1

Unused tech submission

Bring working gadgets — tablets, chargers, adapters — straight to Tech Library counters set up in local Schools, Colleges and RWAs. No travelling across town.

Drop-off
2

Condition rating & quality check

Our technical team inspects the device's physical condition and estimated lifespan, then assigns a Condition Rating — Grade A for near-new, Grade B for fully functional.

Inspection
3

Rental listing & owner incentives

The inspected gadget goes into the short-term rental catalog for students and community members. Every time it's rented out, the original owner earns rewards.

Listing
4

Mandatory data wiping

Every device undergoes a mandatory 100% data-wiping protocol before it's handed out — zero data security risk, guaranteed, no exceptions.

Privacy
5

Dead tech collection

Completely dead, broken or unrepairable electronics don't go in the trash — they go into neighbourhood Green Drop Boxes instead.

Recovery
6

Harvesting & DIY STEM kits

Local repair mechanics salvage usable micro-components — motors, LEDs, switches, wires — and repurpose them into low-cost DIY STEM kits for underprivileged students.

Reuse
